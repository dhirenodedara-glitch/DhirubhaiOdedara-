Build a modern, highly engaging mobile app called VibeUp – Your AI Life & Social Coach, designed primarily for young adults aged 16–30.

1. Main Concept

VibeUp helps young people improve their confidence, social life, productivity, relationships, appearance, communication and personal development through an AI-powered personalised experience.

The app should feel modern, addictive in a healthy way, visually attractive and easy to use.

2. Core Features

AI Personal Coach

- Personal AI coach available 24/7.
- Users choose their goals: confidence, fitness, study, career, relationships, money, social skills or productivity.
- AI creates personalised daily recommendations.
- Daily motivational messages.
- Personal progress tracking.

Daily Glow-Up
Give users a daily checklist such as:

- Exercise
- Drink water
- Study/work goal
- Social challenge
- Self-care
- Reading
- Sleep target
- Confidence challenge

Users earn XP and maintain streaks.

AI Conversation Coach
Allow users to practise:

- Job interviews
- Dating conversations
- Making friends
- Difficult conversations
- Presentations
- Networking

AI should score communication and provide suggestions.

AI Profile & Photo Coach
Users can upload a photo and receive suggestions for:

- Profile-picture quality
- Outfit ideas
- Hairstyle suggestions
- LinkedIn profile improvements
- Dating-profile improvements
- Social-media bio improvements

Do not make unrealistic claims about attractiveness.

Goal Planner
Users enter a goal such as:
"I want to get a job in 3 months."

AI creates:

- Weekly plan
- Daily tasks
- Progress tracker
- Reminders
- Milestones

Social Challenges
Create optional challenges such as:

- Talk to someone new
- Exercise for 20 minutes
- Learn something new
- Complete a confidence task
- Apply for one job

Users can share achievements without exposing private information.

3. Gamification

Include:

- XP points
- Levels
- Daily streaks
- Badges
- Achievements
- Weekly challenges
- Personal progress charts
- Optional friend leaderboard

Avoid manipulative mechanics and excessive notifications.

4. Monetisation

Use a Freemium + Subscription model.

FREE:

- Basic AI coach
- Limited daily coaching
- Basic challenges
- Basic progress tracking

PREMIUM:

- Unlimited AI coaching
- Advanced personalised plans
- AI conversation practice
- Advanced profile/photo coaching
- Premium challenges
- Detailed progress analytics
- Custom AI coach personality
- Exclusive templates

Suggested pricing:

- £5.99/month
- £49.99/year
- 7-day free trial

Also offer optional one-time purchases for premium challenge packs and specialised career/interview packs.

Do not use deceptive billing or difficult cancellation.

5. App Design

Create a premium Gen-Z-friendly interface.

Style:

- Clean
- Minimal
- Modern
- Dynamic animations
- Rounded cards
- Large typography
- Dark/light mode
- Attractive gradients
- Smooth transitions

Bottom navigation:

Home | Coach | Challenges | Progress | Profile

The Home screen should show:

- Today's goal
- Current streak
- XP
- AI recommendation
- Daily challenge
- Progress summary

6. Personalisation

During onboarding ask:

- Age range
- Main goal
- Current lifestyle
- Interests
- Career/study status
- Preferred coaching style

Allow users to change these later.

7. Notifications

Create useful optional notifications:

- Daily goal reminder
- Streak reminder
- Challenge reminder
- Weekly progress summary

Allow users to completely control notification settings.

8. Technical Requirements

Build the app for:

- iOS
- Android

Include:

- Secure authentication
- Apple/Google sign-in
- User profile
- Cloud database
- Subscription management
- Secure AI API integration
- Analytics
- Error handling
- Privacy controls
- Account deletion
- Data export

Never expose API keys in the mobile application.

9. Safety & Privacy

Protect user data.

Include:

- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- Age-appropriate experience
- Report/block functionality for social features
- Secure photo handling
- Delete-account option
- Clear AI disclaimer

Do not position the AI as a therapist, doctor or financial adviser.

10. Growth Features

Build referral functionality:

"Invite 3 friends → receive 7 days of Premium."

Create shareable achievement cards such as:

"🔥 30-Day VibeUp Streak"

Allow users to share cards to Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat.

11. App Store Positioning

App name:
VibeUp

Tagline:
"Level up your life, one day at a time."

Short description:
Your AI-powered coach for confidence, productivity, career and personal growth.

Create a professional onboarding flow and App Store screenshots designed to maximise conversion.

12. Business Dashboard

Create an admin dashboard showing:

- Total users
- Active users
- Premium subscribers
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Trial conversions
- Churn
- Most-used features
- Daily active users
- Referral performance

Design the architecture so additional AI features and subscription tiers can be added later.

Final Goal

The finished product should look like a real commercial startup, not a basic AI demo.

Prioritise:

1. Excellent user experience
2. Strong retention
3. Genuine user value
4. Subscription conversion
5. Privacy and security
6. Fast performance
7. Viral/shareable features

Create the complete product including UI/UX, screens, navigation, database structure, backend architecture, subscription flow, onboarding and production-ready code.
